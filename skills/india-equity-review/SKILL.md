# India Equity Review

Use ../../references/validation-cases.md, evidence.md, point-in-time.md, risk-detection.md and validation-and-calibration.md. Review the completed work in a separate pass without defending the prior conclusion. This is a specialist role; external analyst involvement is not assumed.

Check issuer identity, as-of timestamps, latest filings, financial units, quarter derivations, per-share dilution, action adjustments, normalized estimates, source-backed governance findings and benchmark comparability. Verify formula version, applicability, inputs and units, DCF discounting and common-equity bridge, score arithmetic and coverage, price-to-target calculations and sizing inputs. Challenge every named-holder claim through the ownership ledger and every material headline through the event ledger.

Challenge each thesis with its strongest counterevidence. Ask whether the catalyst is dated and what price already anticipates. Re-run hard-stop gates and inspect the risk register for missing mechanisms, unsupported likelihoods, weak mitigants, understated residual risk and unrealistic exit assumptions. Examine correlated exposures, fees, taxes and missing portfolio context. Rank findings: BLOCKER prevents recommendation; MATERIAL changes downside/order/size; MINOR affects clarity.

Return PASS, PASS WITH LIMITS or BLOCKED with required fixes. A missing actionable quote blocks a current trade plan but need not block a long term business discussion. Do not silently remove contradictory evidence, narrow the universe to preserve the winner or declare a hypothetical test a market backtest. Final outcome can remain WATCH or REJECT.
