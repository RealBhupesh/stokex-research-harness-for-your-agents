# India Equity Market Intelligence

Read [world-to-stock transmission](../../references/world-to-stock-transmission.md) when external conditions could affect the company. Read [price-move attribution](../../references/price-move-attribution.md) whenever the user asks why a stock rose or fell, or when a material move affects a recommendation. Read [active triggers](../../references/active-research-triggers.md) for monitoring work.

Build a relevance-filtered exposure map, then run price attribution over an exact dated window. Use actual adjusted price and volume data. Compare a stated broad benchmark, sector index and peer basket. Separate facts, calculations, analyst inferences and unresolved residuals.

Return the material-driver table, relative-return decomposition, event timeline, competing explanations, confidence labels, decision impact and refresh triggers. Do not force a cause, include unrelated world news, fabricate flow data or treat technical correlation as fundamental causation.
