# Evidence and freshness
Primary authority: exchange-filed issuer disclosures, audited annual reports and notes, quarterly results, shareholding/pledge disclosures, corporate actions; RBI and SEBI for their rules and releases. Company presentations explain management claims but do not override audited statements. A discovery platform is useful for screening, not final authority for a material financial claim.

Every material field needs source ID, URL or supplied-file path, issuer/ISIN, document title, page/table, financial period, publication timestamp when known, retrieval timestamp, units, consolidated/standalone basis, reported/normalized/estimated status and confidence. Save extraction and normalization decisions. Retain only assets licensed for your intended use; public availability does not equal open licensing.

## Run-specific freshness policy, configurable
- Trade setups: latest completed daily session for EOD analysis; executable entry discussion requires a current session quote with exchange timestamp and stated delay. EOD is not live.
- Long term: latest completed session price, latest filed quarter, latest available annual report and subsequent material announcements.
- Corporate actions, suspension, ASM/GSM, price bands and trading series: refresh before final action discussion and again before any later execution.
- Event calendar: check during every run. Expected date is not confirmed date.
- Taxes, settlement, margin and broker fees: verify current official/provider sources when relevant to the decision.

If access fails, try the other exchange or issuer IR, then request the specific missing filing/export. Never bypass access controls. When a latest document cannot be confirmed, disclose the gap rather than using an arbitrary age threshold to declare it fresh.

Reconcile amended versus original filings, currency, crore/lakh/rupee scaling, fiscal versus calendar years, adjusted versus unadjusted prices, gross versus net debt and parent versus group. Read PDF tables visually when extraction is ambiguous. Prefer latest corrected authoritative filing and record the correction. A disagreement affecting valuation or governance blocks a final recommendation until resolved or explicitly bounded by scenarios.

Evidence confidence is separate from investment attractiveness. Facts, management guidance, analyst assumptions and inferences must be distinguishable. No invented consensus or assigned scenario probabilities disguised as measured forecasts.
