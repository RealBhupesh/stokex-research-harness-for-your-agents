# India Equity Screen

Read ../../references/intake.md, evidence.md, point-in-time.md, sectors.md, scoring.md, factors-and-regime.md and forensics-and-traps.md. Define the dated universe and source coverage before screening. Default broad starting universe is current Nifty 500 constituents when a verified list is available; use Nifty 200 or another user-defined scope if appropriate. Never claim an all-India exhaustive screen from a handful of web searches.

Deduplicate ISINs. Separate eligibility gates from ranking. Exclude user-prohibited sectors, suspended securities and mandates with infeasible liquidity. Flag surveillance and governance issues for explicit review rather than claiming they prove fraud. Nonfinancial quality screen: normalize growth, returns, cash conversion, leverage and dilution over several years; use the sector overlay for financials and cyclicals. Trading screen: dated price structure, relative strength, liquidity, volatility and catalyst risk.

Triage in stages: broad eligibility, approximate factor screen, forensic/trap gates, primary-source verification of leading candidates, expectations analysis and deep comparison. Preserve raw factor values and interaction flags; do not hide missing data through renormalization. Publish number examined and omissions. Target three to five comparable finalists if enough qualify, not a compulsory count. Include rejection reasons for plausible alternatives and the relevant benchmark alternative. Fill ../../templates/shortlist.md. No final recommendation from discovery ratios alone.
