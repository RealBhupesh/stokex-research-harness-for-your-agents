# India market calendar

These are recurring events that move Indian stocks, sectors and the index. Timings are typical patterns, not guarantees. Exchanges, regulators and statistics offices change schedules, so **verify each date at use time** from the named source. Record every dated event inside a trade's holding window in the [catalyst calendar](../templates/catalyst-calendar.md) and the trade-ideas "Next-10-sessions event risks" section ([trade ideas](../templates/trade-ideas.md)).

## Corporate and exchange events

| Event | Typical timing | What it moves | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Apr–Jun) results | Mid-July to mid-August | Stock gaps, sector read-through | NSE/BSE board-meeting intimations and results filings |
| Q2 (Jul–Sep) results | Mid-October to mid-November | Same | Same |
| Q3 (Oct–Dec) results | Mid-January to mid-February | Same | Same |
| Q4 (Jan–Mar) and annual results | Mid-April to late May | Same, plus dividends and guidance for the year | Same |
| SEBI results deadline | LODR Reg 33: within 45 days of quarter end for Q1–Q3, and 60 days for audited Q4/annual (verify; SEBI sometimes grants extensions) | Late filers draw scrutiny | SEBI LODR text and circulars |
| Board-meeting intimation | A few working days before the meeting; LODR sets the minimum notice (verify) | Sets the event date for your risk calendar | NSE "Board Meetings" / corporate filings; BSE corporate announcements |
| Trading-window closure | Usually from quarter end until 48 hours after results | Pauses promoter and insider buying | Company intimation on NSE/BSE |
| Con-calls | Same day to about a week after results | Guidance and tone; peer read-through | Company intimation (Reg 30) and transcript filing |
| Shareholding pattern | Within about 21 days of quarter end (verify) | Ownership trend, pledges | NSE/BSE shareholding pattern pages |
| MF monthly portfolios | Within about 10 days of month end (verify) | MF buying and selling by stock | AMC websites via AMFI |
| AMFI monthly flows | Around the second week of the month | SIP and equity-flow context, AMC stocks | AMFI monthly data |
| AGMs and record dates | Mostly July–September, with Q4 dividend record dates | Ex-date price adjustment | NSE corporate actions |
| IPO lock-in expiries | 30/90 days and 6/18 months after listing for anchors, pre-IPO holders and promoters (verify per RHP) | Supply overhang | RHP/prospectus, lock-in notices |
| F&O expiry | Monthly and weekly contracts. The expiry weekday changed in 2025 (NSE moved to Tuesday, BSE to Thursday); verify the current day and holiday shifts | Pinning, rollover, volatility in F&O stocks | NSE and BSE F&O circulars, contract specification pages |
| F&O ban list | Daily, before the open | Blocks new F&O positions; overheated names | NSE "Securities in ban period" |
| F&O stock inclusion/exclusion | Periodic NSE circulars under SEBI eligibility criteria | Liquidity, arbitrage demand | NSE F&O circulars |
| ASM/GSM list changes | Daily or weekly updates | Margin and trading restrictions | NSE/BSE surveillance pages |
| Nifty index rebalance | Semi-annual. Cutoff data to end-January and end-July; changes announced about 4 weeks ahead; effective after the close near the last trading day of March and September (verify) | Passive flows in added and deleted names | niftyindices.com press releases |
| Sensex/BSE index review | Semi-annual, typically effective in June and December (verify) | Passive flows | BSE/Asia Index announcements |
| MSCI index reviews | Quarterly. Announced around the second week of February, May, August and November; effective at the close of the last business day of that month (verify) | Large FPI passive flows | msci.com index review page |
| FTSE reviews | Quarterly (March, June, September, December); semi-annual reviews carry larger changes (verify) | FPI passive flows | FTSE Russell announcements |
| Market holidays | Annual NSE list; special sessions (for example Muhurat trading on Diwali) | Settlement dates, record-date math | NSE holiday circular |
| Settlement | T+1 for cash equities | Last buy date for record-date eligibility | NSE clearing (NCL) circulars |

## Macro and sector data (India)

| Event | Typical timing | What it moves | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly auto sales | 1st of the month (company press releases and exchange filings) | Auto OEMs and ancillaries, tyres | NSE/BSE filings of each OEM; SIAM mid-month; FADA retail around the first week |
| GST collections | Around the 1st of the month (release practice has varied, so verify) | Consumption proxy, market sentiment | Ministry of Finance / GST Council / PIB |
| HSBC India PMI | Manufacturing on the first working day; services around the third | Cyclicals, sentiment | S&P Global release |
| CPI inflation | Around the 12th, about 5:30 pm IST (verify) | Rates, banks, rate-sensitive stocks | MoSPI |
| IIP | Monthly; the release date changed recently (verify on the MoSPI calendar) | Industrial and capital-goods sentiment | MoSPI advance release calendar |
| WPI | Around the 14th | Input costs | Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT |
| Core sector (eight industries) | Around the 20th (verify) | Cement, steel, power | Office of the Economic Adviser |
| Trade data | Around the 15th | INR, exporters | Ministry of Commerce |
| GDP | Quarterly, end-February/May/August/November | Broad market | MoSPI |
| RBI MPC | Six meetings a year (bi-monthly); decision about 10:00 am IST on the final day | Banks, NBFCs, real estate, autos, bond yields | RBI MPC schedule and press release |
| RBI sectoral credit, bank credit/deposits | Fortnightly and monthly | Banks, NBFC read-through | RBI DBIE / press releases |
| FII/DII provisional data | Daily after the close | Regime context | NSE FII/DII report; NSDL FPI daily and fortnightly |
| DGCA air traffic | Monthly | Aviation, airports | DGCA |
| Power demand | Daily and monthly | Utilities, coal | Grid-India / CEA |
| Port cargo, rail freight | Monthly | Logistics, ports, cement, coal | IPA, Indian Railways |
| Union Budget | 1 February (an interim budget in election years) | Sectors via capex, taxes, duties, PLI | indiabudget.gov.in, PIB |
| GST Council meetings | Irregular | Rate changes by product | GST Council, PIB |
| IMD monsoon forecasts | First forecast mid-April; update late May/June; onset near 1 June; season June–September | Agri inputs, rural consumption, FMCG, two-wheelers, tractors | IMD |
| Kharif and rabi sowing | Weekly during the season | Agri chemicals, fertilisers, seeds | Ministry of Agriculture |
| Sugar season | October–September; ISMA estimates; export and ethanol policy | Sugar stocks | ISMA, DFPD notifications |
| Festive demand | Navratri–Diwali (September–November, date shifts yearly); wedding seasons | Autos, retail, jewellery, durables; YoY base effects | Company updates, FADA, GST |
| Quarterly business updates | First week after quarter end (banks, NBFCs, retailers, some FMCG) | Pre-results read-through | NSE/BSE Reg 30 filings |

|---|---|---|---|
| US FOMC decision | 8 meetings a year; statement at 11:30 pm IST (US daylight time) or 12:30 am IST (US standard time) | Global risk, FII flows, INR, IT/financials the next day | Federal Reserve calendar |

| Accenture results | Late March, June, September, December (fiscal year ends August) | Indian IT services | Accenture IR |

| MSCI/FTSE effective days | Month-end close | FPI passive trades at the closing auction | Index providers |

## How to build the next-10-sessions event calendar

1. Set `as_of` (IST) and list the next 10 NSE trading dates from the holiday circular. Mark the F&O expiry day or days in that window from the current NSE/BSE contract calendar.
2. For each candidate symbol, search NSE/BSE announcements for board-meeting intimations, record dates, AGM/EGM, buyback or QIP windows, lock-in expiries and scheduled con-calls. Record the broadcast time.
3. Check whether the candidate is in any pending index change (Nifty, Sensex, MSCI, FTSE) or F&O inclusion/exclusion circular.
4. Add sector data releases that fall in the window, such as auto sales on the 1st for autos, or MPC for banks and NBFCs. Use the tables above and [sector KPIs](sector-kpis.md).
5. Add macro and global events in the window: MPC, CPI, FOMC, US CPI, Budget, GST Council, major peer results.
6. Tag each event BINARY (results, court verdict, regulatory decision), SCHEDULED DATA, or FLOW (index/expiry). Binary events inside the holding window require one of: an explicit event trade, reduced size, or exit before the event.
7. Write the calendar into the trade plan. An unconfirmed date is labelled `UNCONFIRMED` and never treated as fact.
