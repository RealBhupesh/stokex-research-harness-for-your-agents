# Legal and credit intelligence

Search official exchange disclosures, SEBI orders, court and tribunal records, IBBI and NCLT material, rating rationales, lender disclosures and company filings when relevant.

Check rating and outlook changes, covenant pressure, refinancing maturity, defaults, guarantees, contingent liabilities, insolvency applications, material litigation, regulatory investigation, tax disputes, environmental permissions and promoter or group obligations.

For every issue record legal entity, proceeding or instrument, authority, case or rating identifier, event date, publication date, claimed exposure, company response, source IDs, severity, status and scenario impact. Distinguish allegation, proceeding, order and final outcome.

Open material issues require quantified or ranged downside where possible. Unclear exposure remains `UNKNOWN`. Do not provide legal conclusions beyond the source record. A material unresolved issue can force `RETURN_FOR_WORK`, `VETO`, `REJECT` or `INSUFFICIENT DATA`.
