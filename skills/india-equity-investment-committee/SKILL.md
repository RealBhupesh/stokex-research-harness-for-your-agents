# India Equity Investment Committee

Read [investment committee](../../references/investment-committee.md), [legal and credit intelligence](../../references/legal-credit-intelligence.md), [risk detection](../../references/risk-detection.md) and [portfolio context](../../references/portfolio-context.md). Review the completed artifacts as a skeptic.

Test the strongest alternative explanation, priced-in expectations, accounting normalization, forecast bridge, scenario coherence, catalyst failure, management incentives, legal-credit exposure, liquidity, portfolio fit and opportunity cost. Trace every response to evidence.

Return the challenge record, unresolved items, conditions, research status and one IC action. `APPROVE_FOR_CONSIDERATION` and `APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS` require every mandatory gate to pass. A speculative case additionally requires capped capital at risk, explicit acceptance of total-loss risk, a dated catalyst, passed liquidity and pre-event and post-event exit plans.

Do not repair the thesis during review, average away a blocker or approve because another analyst or investor owns the stock.
