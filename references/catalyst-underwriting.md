# Catalyst underwriting

A catalyst must change an expectation, cash flow, discount rate, risk perception or demand for shares. A date alone is not a catalyst.

For each catalyst record event, expected window, affected market belief, evidence, leading indicators, positive/base/negative interpretations, probability only when defensible, likely time to recognition, what may already be priced, failure mode, delay rule and review trigger.

Build an event tree for time-bound and speculative mandates. Model success, partial success, delay and failure. Connect each branch to forecast, valuation, liquidity and downside. Check whether management controls the outcome and whether the company has enough financial runway for delay.

Reject a time-bound thesis with no recognition mechanism. Long-duration compounders may instead use milestone-based monitoring, but valuation and opportunity cost remain controlling.
