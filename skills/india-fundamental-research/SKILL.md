# India Fundamental Research

Read ../../references/evidence.md, expert-rules.md, sectors.md and formulas.md. Collect latest quarter, latest annual report including notes, recent announcements and ownership/encumbrance history. Read ../../references/ownership.md when named holders matter and ../../references/news.md for the current event map. Prefer at least five annual periods and eight quarters where available; fewer periods require disclosed uncertainty, not fabricated history.

Explain revenue drivers, customer concentration, pricing, competitive position, reinvestment runway and capital allocation. Build a revenue/margin/capital intensity driver bridge. Check organic versus acquisition growth, segment shifts, cyclicality and management guidance history. Normalize standalone and consolidated data deliberately. Quarterly cash flows may not be filed each quarter; do not invent them.

Trace PAT to CFO, working capital and free cash flow; distinguish maintenance and expansion capex with sourced estimates. Inspect contingent liabilities, lease treatment, auditor qualifications, related-party transactions, dilution and promoter encumbrances. Separate unresolved red flags from established misconduct.

Write three evidence-backed thesis pillars, strongest countercase, measurable prove/kill conditions, and what current price appears to require. Use only applicable diagnostic formulas and disclose their input periods and limitations. Route valuation to ../india-equity-valuation/SKILL.md. A quality business at an unattractive price can be WATCH. A celebrated holder does not make the thesis true. Dividend yield alone is not income safety; inspect payout, cash flow and balance sheet. Produce source-linked financial worksheet and recommendation template.
