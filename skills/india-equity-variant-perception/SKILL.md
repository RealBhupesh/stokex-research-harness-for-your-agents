# India Equity Variant Perception

Begin only after the evidence and normalization gates pass. Read [variant perception](../../references/variant-perception.md), [forecast policy](../../references/forecast-policy.md) and [catalyst underwriting](../../references/catalyst-underwriting.md). Read [management credibility](../../references/management-credibility.md) when guidance informs the forecast and [relationship graph](../../references/relationship-graph.md) when external entities drive the thesis.

Build explicit operating-driver forecasts and preserve estimate vintages. Compare them with sourced consensus or clearly labeled reverse-valuation expectations. State the material difference, evidence, counterevidence and path to recognition.

Return the driver bridge, scenario statements, expectations map, variant claims, management ledger and catalyst tree. If no material variant or credible path exists, do not promote the company to a research candidate. Do not invent probabilities, consensus, customers or management access.
