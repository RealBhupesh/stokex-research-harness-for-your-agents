# Indian market constraints
Verified source pointers are in `research-sources.md`. These controls are distinct from the workflow's configurable screening heuristics.

- Verify legal issuer, ISIN, NSE symbol, BSE code and listing series; dual listings are one company, not two independent ideas.
- Check current suspension, ASM/GSM stage, trade-for-trade status, applicable price band and permitted trading frequency. Surveillance inclusion is not proof of fraud, but restrictions can defeat entry/exit assumptions.
- Inspect promoter ownership, encumbrances/pledges and changes across recent quarters; reconcile relevant filings rather than assuming a screen's pledge field is current. High pledging is a risk requiring context, not automatic proof of wrongdoing.
- Test traded value, spread, market depth where available, order size participation and circuit risk. A stop cannot guarantee execution, especially after a gap or circuit lock.
- Distinguish corporate-action adjusted price returns from total returns including dividends. Reconcile splits, bonus issues, rights, demergers and buybacks before calculating momentum or per-share value.
- Verify current settlement and broker treatment for the exact security. Do not assume optional faster settlement applies to every stock or account.
- Model brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, stamp duty, applicable regulatory charges, DP charges and slippage from current official/broker schedules. Buy and sell charges can differ. Taxes are not a universal flat haircut per winning trade.
- Research-date tax reference: official CBDT pages describe specified listed-equity STCG under section 111A at 20%, and section 112A LTCG at 12.5% above the applicable ₹1.25 lakh annual threshold, subject to conditions, effective dates, cess/surcharge and individual circumstances. Confirm the currently applicable Act, section numbering, holding rules and transition provisions for the disposal date. This is a reference, not a personal tax calculation. Intraday/derivative business-income treatment requires separate verification.
- Default scope is mainboard cash equities. SME/illiquid/new listings require a separate opt-in mandate, lot-size and exit analysis, and explicit uncertainty. No unrequested leverage or derivatives. No promises of assured returns.
