# Adversarial investment committee

Review the completed case without defending the draft. The committee decides whether the evidence, variant, reward and risk justify consideration within the mandate.

Challenge:

1. strongest bear case and alternative explanation;
2. whether the thesis is already priced;
3. normalization and forecast linkage;
4. valuation dependence on terminal or exit assumptions;
5. catalyst mechanism, delay and failure;
6. management incentives and minority-shareholder treatment;
7. legal, credit, audit, liquidity and portfolio constraints;
8. coherent stress loss and exit feasibility;
9. cheaper or safer alternatives;
10. anchoring to purchase price, management, notable holders or recent price action.

Record each challenge, response, evidence and residual. Classify findings as `BLOCKER`, `MATERIAL` or `MINOR`. Any unresolved controlling contradiction or hard stop prevents approval.

IC actions:

- `APPROVE_FOR_CONSIDERATION`: required gates pass; sizing remains conditional on portfolio inputs.
- `APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS`: named conditions must be verified before action.
- `RETURN_FOR_WORK`: fixable evidence, model or contradiction gap remains.
- `VETO`: mandate breach, unacceptable downside or hard risk.

No score can override a veto.
