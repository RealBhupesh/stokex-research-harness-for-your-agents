# Analyst performance lab

Evaluate the process using frozen, point-in-time decisions. Preserve rejected and watch cases to reduce selection bias.

Track forecast error by metric and horizon, scenario coverage, probability calibration, decision outcome, excess return, drawdown, catalyst timing, execution difference and thesis-state discipline. Attribute errors to evidence, normalization, industry, forecast, valuation, catalyst, management, legal-credit, risk, portfolio, execution or process.

Compare results with a stated base-rate or benchmark forecast. Segment by mandate, horizon, sector, market regime and confidence, but publish sample sizes. Do not optimize thresholds on the same sample used to report results.

Use walk-forward or genuinely held-out evaluation. Include costs, taxes, delisted securities, survivorship, availability timestamps and historical universe membership. A small sample produces a warning and cannot automatically rewrite the skill.
