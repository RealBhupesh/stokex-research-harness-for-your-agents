# Forecast policy

Forecast from operating drivers, not a trend line. Begin with reconciled reported or normalized history. Build revenue from sector-relevant units such as volume, price, capacity, utilization, customers, ticket size or loan balances. Build margins from price, mix, input cost, operating leverage and fixed-cost assumptions. Link working capital, capex, financing, tax, dilution and cash flow.

For every material input record source or derivation, low and high range, historical base, transmission mechanism, correlation with other inputs, forecast horizon and disconfirming indicator. Prevent double-counting related drivers.

Create bear, base and bull statements that reconcile operating assumptions with earnings, cash flow, balance sheet and valuation. Probabilities are optional. If used, they must be explicit, sum to 100% and remain visibly assumption-driven. Never set the user's desired return as the base case.

Track estimate vintages. Explain revisions through changed drivers and separate information change from model correction. Use ranges where evidence cannot support point precision.
