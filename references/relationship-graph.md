# Company relationship graph

Map the entities that transmit economics or risk to the listed security: promoters, subsidiaries, associates, joint ventures, material customers, suppliers, lenders, rating agencies, competitors, distributors and regulators.

For each edge record legal entity, relationship type, economic dependency, concentration where disclosed, source IDs, reporting date, related-party status and potential read-through signal. Do not infer an exact customer or supplier from an unattributed article.

Use the graph to detect concentration, circular transactions, cross-guarantees, promoter-group exposure, refinancing channels and competitor or supplier-customer read-throughs. A market reaction elsewhere is a hypothesis, not proof of the listed company's outcome.
