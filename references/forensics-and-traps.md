# Forensic accounting and speculation traps

These are investigation gates, not accusations and not an additive “fraud probability.” Composite scores can hide one fatal issue, so report each flag with severity, evidence, alternative explanation and resolution.

## Accounting checks

- PAT versus CFO and free cash flow over a cycle, including tax and working-capital mechanics.
- Receivables, unbilled revenue and contract assets versus revenue, with ageing and expected-credit-loss policy.
- Inventory versus sales/COGS, obsolescence and channel stuffing indicators where relevant.
- Capitalized costs, CWIP, intangible additions, depreciation policy and recurring “exceptional” items.
- Other income, related-party revenue/purchases/loans/guarantees and subsidiary cash movements.
- Auditor qualification/emphasis, resignation, fee relationship, delayed results and restatements.
- Contingent liabilities, guarantees, tax/litigation exposure, debt maturity, covenant and rating changes.
- Equity dilution, warrants, convertibles, ESOP overhang, preferential allotments and use of proceeds.
- Promoter pledges/encumbrances, selling, reclassification and governance changes.

Compare rates as well as absolute growth. For example, receivables growing faster than revenue is a prompt to inspect terms and ageing, not proof of manipulation. Use Beneish only when line-item mappings are valid and show all components.

## Speculation and exit-risk gates

Refresh ASM/GSM, trade-to-trade, SME status, price band, suspension, free float, average traded value, spread/depth when available, upper/lower circuit frequency, preferential issues/warrants, promoter activity, auditor changes, exchange news clarification and cash-flow quality.

Classify:

- BLOCKED: evidence or market structure makes a recommendation unreliable or exit assumptions indefensible.
- EXTREME SPECULATION: a tail outcome may exist, but business evidence, valuation, liquidity or event uncertainty makes loss and exit risk dominant.
- SPECULATIVE RESEARCH CANDIDATE: explicit catalyst and trade plan exist, but downside and uncertainty remain high.
- ORDINARY RESEARCH CANDIDATE: standard gates pass for the selected mandate.

Never convert circuit frequency, delivery percentage, social-media activity or sudden volume into evidence of informed accumulation. A planned stop is not executable during a locked circuit or overnight gap.
