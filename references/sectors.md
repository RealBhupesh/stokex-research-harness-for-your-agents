# Sector-specific interpretation
| Sector | Essential operating evidence | Valuation emphasis | Common trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banks | NIM, credit cost, GNPA/NNPA, provisions, deposit mix, capital adequacy, growth | P/B linked to sustainable ROE; equity/residual income model | Applying industrial net debt or FCFF to deposits |
| NBFC | ALM, borrowing mix, collection and delinquency cohorts, capital, concentration | P/B and earnings with credit-cycle stress | Treating disbursement growth as asset quality |
| Insurance | Segment economics, solvency, persistency, VNB/embedded value definitions | EV and VNB where meaningful | Mixing life and general-insurance metrics |
| IT services | Organic constant-currency growth, utilization, margins, client concentration, cash | P/E and DCF with demand scenarios | Treating bookings as current revenue |
| Consumer | Volume vs price/mix, distribution, working capital, input costs | Earnings/FCF, reinvestment runway | Revenue growth masking volume decline |
| Industrials | Order book quality, execution, cash collection, capacity and capex | Normalized earnings/FCF | Orders treated as cash or guaranteed revenue |
| Commodities | Unit costs, cycle, leverage, capacity, commodity/FX sensitivity | Mid-cycle EV/EBITDA and stress asset values | Peak earnings look cheapest |
| Pharma | Product concentration, approvals, inspections, litigation, R&D | Segment-aware earnings/DCF | Unconfirmed regulatory success in base case |
| Realty | Collections, presales, completion, project debt, land and obligations | Project/NAV and cash flow cross-check | Presales equated with revenue or profit |
| Utilities | Tariffs, receivables, regulated returns, fuel supply, capex debt | Regulated economics and DCF | Ignoring cash collection and refinancing |
| Holding companies | Listed/unlisted holdings, parent debt, tax leakage, governance | SOTP with defensible discount | Double-counting subsidiaries and debt |

Define every KPI using issuer disclosure. Do not compare incompatible fiscal periods or accounting definitions. Sector exclusions and specialist data needs belong in intake. REITs/InvITs need a separate distribution, debt and asset-value framework rather than ordinary company P/E rankings.
