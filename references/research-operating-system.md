# Research operating system

STOCKEX separates research into gated artifacts so a polished narrative cannot hide missing work.

| Stage | Required output | Blocking condition |
|---|---|---|
| Mandate | objective, horizon, loss and liquidity constraints | objective or horizon unknown |
| Universe | reproducible inclusion and exclusion log | winner chosen before universe |
| Evidence | point-in-time source ledger and conflicts | controlling evidence missing |
| Market intelligence | material transmission map and move attribution | fabricated cause or stale market data |
| Business | economics, industry and peer dossier | business drivers not understood |
| Normalization | reconciled reported history and adjustments | units, periods or cash flow unresolved |
| Expectations | market-implied view and independent variant | no defensible difference |
| Forecast and valuation | coherent scenarios and sensitivities | inconsistent assumptions |
| Market structure | price, volume, liquidity and execution evidence | exit not feasible |
| Risk | layered register and hard stops | unresolved controlling risk |
| Catalysts | mechanism, window and failure path | no recognition path for time-bound thesis |
| Decision | alternatives, sizing constraints and status | reward does not justify risk |
| IC | adversarial challenge record | blocker unresolved |
| Monitoring | triggers and frozen prediction record | no invalidation or review plan |

Permitted research statuses are `RESEARCH CANDIDATE`, `SPECULATIVE RESEARCH CANDIDATE`, `WATCH`, `REJECT`, `SCREEN ONLY` and `INSUFFICIENT DATA`. No obligation exists to select a stock.
