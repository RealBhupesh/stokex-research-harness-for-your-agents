# India Equity Thesis Monitor

Read [thesis monitoring](../../references/thesis-monitoring.md), [active triggers](../../references/active-research-triggers.md) and [analyst performance lab](../../references/analyst-performance-lab.md).

Load the frozen prediction record and latest evidence packet. Classify new information, identify affected assumptions and rerun only the necessary research stages. Preserve prior versions and require a dated source and reason for every state change.

Return the new thesis version, status transition, forecast and valuation changes, risk and liquidity changes, next review and unresolved work. On closure, complete a post-mortem that attributes errors to the research process. Do not equate price outcome with thesis quality or change the framework from one winner or loser.
