# Active research triggers

Active means the research packet defines refresh triggers and reruns affected stages when new data is available. It does not imply continuous monitoring without a configured feed or automation.

| Trigger | Rerun |
|---|---|
| Results, guidance or material filing | evidence, normalization, forecast, valuation, risk, IC |
| Auditor, management, promoter or rating change | evidence, governance, legal-credit, risk, IC |
| Unusual benchmark-relative price or volume | attribution, market structure, risk |
| Thesis-critical commodity, currency or rate move | transmission map, forecast, valuation, risk |
| Regulation, court or tax decision | evidence, transmission map, scenarios, risk |
| Competitor capacity, pricing or technology event | industry, forecast, variant view |
| Catalyst delay or failure | catalyst map, probability, valuation, decision |
| Liquidity, circuit or surveillance change | execution, sizing, risk, IC |

Every trigger stores occurrence time, observed time, source ID, affected assumptions, old state, new state and reviewer. Without automation, output a dated manual refresh checklist.
