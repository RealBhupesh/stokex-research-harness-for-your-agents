# Evidence gates

Every material claim receives a source ID, publisher, URL or local document reference, source class, publication time, availability time, period covered, exact location where practical, fact-versus-inference label, corroboration state and limitation.

## Authority order

1. Regulator, exchange, court and government records.
2. Audited annual reports and filed results.
3. Filed company announcements and presentations.
4. Official industry and macroeconomic data.
5. Licensed or documented market data.
6. Rating rationales and credible secondary research.
7. News and social material as discovery or perception evidence.

Use weaker sources only when a stronger equivalent is unavailable and disclose the downgrade. A secondary story cannot resolve an exchange-filing contradiction.

## Point-in-time rule

Record when information became available, not only the period it describes. Evidence published after the research cutoff cannot support that historical decision. Preserve amended and original versions. Record missing filings and blocked tools rather than guessing.

## Gate result

- `PASS`: every controlling claim is adequately sourced and conflicts are resolved.
- `PASS_WITH_LIMITS`: non-controlling gaps remain and are disclosed.
- `FAIL`: a controlling source is missing, future-dated, stale for the decision or materially contradictory.

`FAIL` forces `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `WATCH` or `REJECT`.
