# World-to-stock transmission

Include an external driver only when evidence connects it to revenue, costs, assets, liabilities, financing, valuation, ownership flows, liquidity or market expectations. Mark the rest `NOT MATERIAL`.

## Driver map

Check global growth and risk appetite; interest rates, yields, credit and liquidity; relevant currencies; commodities, power and freight; Indian consumption, investment, credit and fiscal policy; geopolitics, sanctions, tariffs and trade routes; regulation and courts; technology substitution; weather and seasonality; industry capacity, inventories and pricing; verified FPI, DII, insider and passive flows; and company events.

For each material driver record:

- current state, source IDs and data timestamp;
- direct or indirect company exposure;
- transmission chain and expected lag;
- direction and magnitude range;
- leading indicator and countervailing force;
- affected forecast line, valuation input, risk or catalyst;
- confidence and disconfirming evidence.

Do not equate a macro forecast with a stock forecast. Test sensitivity in bear, base and bull cases. Use primary sources such as RBI, MoSPI, ministries, regulators, exchanges and company filings before commentary. Use IMF, World Bank, WTO and relevant official foreign agencies for global inputs.

## Decision rule

A driver changes the decision only if its plausible effect is material to the stated horizon. If the path is real but magnitude is unknown, retain it as a risk with `UNKNOWN` likelihood rather than inventing precision.
