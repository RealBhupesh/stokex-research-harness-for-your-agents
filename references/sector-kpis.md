# Sector KPIs

This is a compact sector overlay for Indian listed equities. It extends [sectors](sectors.md) with the lead KPIs, the data releases that move each sector, what drives short-term price action, the usual valuation anchor and the classic trap. Define every KPI from the issuer's own disclosure: results, investor presentation, con-call and AR. Verify release timings in the [market calendar](india-market-calendar.md). Valuation anchors are conventions, not fair-value rules.

## Private banks

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - Loan and deposit growth; CASA ratio; cost of deposits.
  - NIM, credit cost, slippage ratio, GNPA/NNPA.
  - Provision coverage, restructured book, unsecured retail share.
  - CRAR/CET1, LCR, credit-deposit ratio.
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates in the first week after quarter end (Reg 30); RBI fortnightly bank credit/deposits; RBI MPC; RBI circulars (risk weights, ECL, LCR).
- **Short-term movers:**
  - Deposit growth against loan growth in the business update.
  - NIM guidance on the con-call.
  - Rate cuts, which compress NIM for repo-linked books.
  - Asset-quality surprises in unsecured loans and microfinance (MFI).
  - FII flows, since private banks are large FII holdings.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/B against sustainable ROE; P/ABV for stressed lenders.
- **Trap:** loan growth funded by high-cost deposits; a low GNPA hiding a rising restructured or SMA book; one-time treasury gains.

## PSU banks

- **Lead KPIs:** as for private banks, plus pension and wage provisions, recoveries from written-off accounts, treasury gains/losses (bond yields), government stake and CET1 headroom.
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates; RBI data; government recapitalisation, OFS and QIP news; bond yields.
- **Short-term movers:**
  - Credit-cost surprises.
  - Treasury income as yields move.
  - Dividend payouts to government.
  - QIP pricing.
  - Government OFS or stake-sale plans (minimum public shareholding compliance).
- **Valuation anchor:** P/B against ROE, with a PSU discount.
- **Trap:** ROE lifted by recoveries and tax write-backs that do not recur; a QIP overhang after a rally.

## NBFCs and housing finance

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - AUM growth by product; disbursements.
  - Yield, cost of funds, spread/NIM.
  - Stage 2 and 3 assets; collection efficiency; write-offs.
  - ALM gaps; borrowing mix (banks, NCDs, CP, ECB); capital adequacy.
  - Credit rating.
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates; RBI risk-weight and scale-based regulation circulars; rating actions; MPC.
- **Short-term movers:** AUM or disbursement updates; rating upgrades or downgrades; RBI actions on specific lenders (embargo orders); cost-of-funds trends as rates move.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/B against ROA/ROE through the cycle.
- **Trap:** disbursement growth taken as asset quality; low credit cost from a young book (unseasoned vintage); evergreening through co-lending or ARC sales.

## Insurance

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Life:** APE growth, VNB and VNB margin, product mix (ULIP / non-par / protection), persistency (13th and 61st month), embedded value.
  - **General/health:** GDPI growth, combined ratio, loss ratio, solvency ratio, market share.
- **Data releases:** monthly premium data from IRDAI and the Life/General Insurance Councils (early in the month); IRDAI regulation changes (surrender value, commission caps, expense of management); budget tax changes.
- **Short-term movers:** monthly APE/premium prints; tax-rule changes on policies; regulatory product changes; bancassurance partner changes.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/EV and implied value of new business for life insurers; P/E or P/B against combined-ratio-driven ROE for general insurers.
- **Trap:** reading GAAP P/E for life insurers; VNB margin gains from mix that regulation can reverse.

## AMCs, brokers and exchanges

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **AMC:** QAAUM, equity share of AUM, SIP flows, yield (bps), market share.
  - **Brokers:** active clients, F&O and cash ADTO, market share, float income.
  - **Exchanges and depositories:** transaction revenue, F&O premium turnover, new demat accounts, regulatory fee changes.
- **Data releases:** AMFI monthly (around the second week); NSE monthly business growth; NSDL/CDSL account data; SEBI circulars (F&O rules, TER changes, true-to-label pricing).
- **Short-term movers:** SEBI regulatory changes (F&O restrictions have hit volumes); monthly AUM, SIP and turnover prints; market direction.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E, and % of AUM for AMCs.
- **Trap:** extrapolating peak F&O volumes; regulatory fee or TER cuts.

## IT services

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - Constant-currency revenue growth, split organic and inorganic.
  - Deal TCV and net new; book-to-bill.
  - Vertical mix (BFSI, retail, hi-tech); top-client growth.
  - EBIT margin; utilisation; attrition; headcount change.
  - Pricing; cash conversion.
- **Data releases:**
  - Quarterly results; TCS usually opens the season in the second week after quarter end.
  - Accenture results (late March/June/September/December).
  - US macro (payrolls, CPI); the INR/USD rate.
  - US visa and outsourcing policy.
- **Short-term movers:**
  - Guidance revisions (Infosys, HCLTech give annual guidance).
  - Large-deal announcements.
  - Accenture bookings and commentary.
  - US tech spending signals; INR moves.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E against growth, relative to peers and history.
- **Trap:** treating TCV as revenue; margin support from INR depreciation or a utilisation spike that reverses; a large deal with front-loaded costs.

## Pharma and CDMO

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - US generics revenue and price erosion; key launches (limited-competition or first-to-file).
  - India formulations growth against the IPM; R&D % of sales.
  - USFDA inspection outcomes (EIR, 483, OAI, warning letter, import alert).
  - **CDMO:** order book, molecule pipeline by phase, capacity utilisation.
- **Data releases:** USFDA inspection databases and warning letters (weekly); monthly IPM data (licensed AIOCD/IQVIA, so treat press summaries as leads); NPPA/DPCO price notifications; US patent litigation.
- **Short-term movers:** USFDA observations and clearances (Reg 30 disclosures); approvals for key products; US biosecure-type policy news for CDMOs; generic-price trends.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E on base business plus optionality for launches; EV/EBITDA for CDMOs.
- **Trap:** a limited-competition product's peak earnings valued as perpetual; an unresolved OAI plant; the CDMO "China+1" narrative without signed contracts.

## Hospitals and diagnostics

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Hospitals:** operational beds, occupancy, ARPOB, payer mix (insurance, cash, government schemes, international), new-bed ramp-up, EBITDA per bed.
  - **Diagnostics:** volume (patients, tests), realisation per test, B2C share, network expansion.
- **Data releases:** quarterly results; state government price-cap proposals; scheme reimbursement changes (Ayushman Bharat, CGHS).
- **Short-term movers:** occupancy and ARPOB prints; new-bed commissioning; price regulation headlines; competitive pricing in diagnostics.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA and EV per bed.
- **Trap:** an ARPOB rise from mix shift that is not repeatable; margin dilution from new hospitals during ramp-up ignored in multiples.

## FMCG

- **Lead KPIs:** underlying volume growth; price/mix; rural against urban growth; gross margin against input costs (palm oil, crude derivatives, wheat, milk, tea, copra); A&P spend; distribution reach; channel inventory.
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates (some companies); monsoon and rural wage data; GST rate changes; commodity prices; NielsenIQ reports (licensed, so press summaries are leads).
- **Short-term movers:** volume surprises; commentary on the rural recovery; input-cost moves (palm oil); GST rate changes.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E against its own long history; FCF yield.
- **Trap:** revenue growth driven by price with volume declining; premium multiples on decelerating volume.

## Consumer durables and retail

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Durables:** volume by category, channel inventory, market share, commodity costs (copper, aluminium, steel), import dependence (PLI localisation).
  - **Retail:** same-store sales growth (SSSG), footprint addition, revenue per sq ft, gross margin, inventory days.
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates (retailers such as Avenue Supermarts and Trent); festive and seasonal data (summer for ACs and coolers); weather; BIS/QCO rules.
- **Short-term movers:** summer heat (ACs), festive sell-through, SSSG prints, store-addition updates.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/sales for early-stage retail.
- **Trap:** channel stuffing before a season; store additions masking weak SSSG.

## Autos and auto ancillaries

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - Domestic volumes by segment (PV, 2W, CV, tractor, 3W) and exports.
  - Market share; inventory days at dealers.
  - ASP and mix (SUV, premium bikes, EV share).
  - Commodity costs (steel, aluminium, precious metals); discounts.
  - **Ancillaries:** content per vehicle, OEM concentration, export share, EV/ICE mix.
- **Data releases:**
  - OEM monthly sales on the 1st (NSE/BSE filings).
  - FADA retail in the first week of the next month; Vahan registrations (daily).
  - SIAM wholesale around mid-month.
  - Tractor sales (1st); EV subsidy scheme changes.
- **Short-term movers:**
  - Monthly volume against expectations.
  - Retail against wholesale divergence.
  - Festival-season bookings.
  - EV policy changes and emission-norm changes.
  - Steel price moves; export-market FX (Africa, Latin America for 2W).
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E, EV/EBITDA; SOTP for groups with listed or financing subsidiaries.
- **Trap:** wholesale dispatch into dealer inventory (check FADA and Vahan); festive shifts distorting YoY; an ancillary valued as an "EV play" with little EV revenue.

## Cement

- **Lead KPIs:** volume and capacity utilisation; realisation per tonne; EBITDA per tonne; power, fuel and freight cost per tonne (petcoke, coal, diesel); capacity additions by region; lead distance.
- **Data releases:** core sector cement output (around the 20th); dealer price checks (sell-side, so leads only); petcoke prices; monsoon (construction slows June–September); quarterly results.
- **Short-term movers:** regional price hikes and rollbacks; fuel-cost moves; consolidation M&A; monsoon and demand commentary.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA and EV per tonne of capacity against replacement cost.
- **Trap:** capacity additions ahead of demand compressing prices; EV/tonne comparisons ignoring asset quality and region.

## Metals and mining

- **Lead KPIs:** volumes; realisations against LME/China/domestic prices; cost per tonne (coking coal, iron ore, power); spreads; net debt/EBITDA; captive raw material; capacity pipeline.
- **Data releases:** LME prices (daily); China PMI, property and steel output data; domestic steel prices (JPC data); safeguard and anti-dumping duties (DGTR, CBIC); iron-ore royalty and export-duty changes; NMDC monthly price and volume releases.
- **Short-term movers:** China stimulus headlines; LME moves; duty changes; coking-coal cost moves; INR.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA on mid-cycle earnings; P/B through the cycle.
- **Trap:** buying at peak earnings because the P/E looks low; ignoring the lag in coking-coal cost pass-through.

## Oil and gas (OMCs, upstream, city gas)

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **OMCs:** gross refining margin (GRM), marketing margin per litre on petrol, diesel and LPG, inventory gains/losses, LPG under-recovery compensation.
  - **Upstream:** realised crude and gas price, production volumes, windfall tax and cess, APM gas price formula.
  - **City gas distribution (CGD):** volume growth (CNG, PNG), APM gas allocation, spread per scm.
- **Data releases:** Brent (daily); Singapore GRM (weekly proxy); PPAC monthly data; government gas pricing notifications (monthly APM price); windfall-tax notifications; retail fuel price changes.
- **Short-term movers:** crude moves (inverse for OMCs); fuel price cuts or hikes; APM gas-allocation cuts (CGD); LPG compensation announcements; OPEC+ decisions.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA and P/B for OMCs; P/E and EV/2P reserves for upstream; P/E against volume growth for CGD.
- **Trap:** annualising a quarter of inventory gains or peak marketing margin; CGD margins that depend on allocation of cheap APM gas.

## Chemicals and specialty chemicals

- **Lead KPIs:** volume against realisation split; product-specific spreads; China pricing and dumping; customer inventory cycles (agrochem, pharma intermediates); capacity utilisation and new-plant ramp; export share; working capital.
- **Data releases:** China chemical prices (licensed or trade press); anti-dumping duty cases (DGTR); global agrochem channel commentary; crude and key feedstock prices.
- **Short-term movers:** China price moves and destocking/restocking commentary; anti-dumping duty imposition; large contract (CSM/CRAMS) announcements.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E and EV/EBITDA against growth; history is less reliable after the 2021 peak multiples.
- **Trap:** capex-led growth that assumes pricing will hold; "China+1" contracts that are MoUs, not firm offtake.

## Capital goods, defence and railways

- **Lead KPIs:** order inflow and order book / TTM revenue; execution rate; margin by segment; working-capital days (government customers pay slowly); advances; indigenisation content (defence); export orders.
- **Data releases:**
  - Reg 30 order announcements.
  - MoD Defence Acquisition Council approvals (which are not orders) and contract signings.
  - Railway budget and tender results.
  - The Union Budget capex allocation and monthly central government capex (CGA data).
- **Short-term movers:** large order wins; DAC approvals; budget allocations; export deals; quarterly inflow guidance.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E against order-book visibility; EV/EBITDA.
- **Trap:** order book treated as cash; L1 status counted as a win; working capital consuming the profit (CFO/PAT); extreme multiples on thematic flows.

## Power, utilities and renewables

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Regulated utilities:** regulated equity, PLF/PAF, fuel availability, receivables (DISCOM dues, LPS scheme).
  - **Renewables:** capacity commissioned against pipeline, CUF, PPA tariffs, module and cell costs, ALMM/DCR rules.
  - **Transmission:** projects won (TBCB), capitalisation.
  - **Merchant power:** exchange prices (IEX).
- **Data releases:** Grid-India and CEA daily/monthly peak demand; IEX monthly volumes and prices; MNRE capacity data; CERC/SERC tariff orders; coal stocks at plants.
- **Short-term movers:** peak-demand records in heatwaves; merchant power prices; tender wins; market-coupling news (for IEX); policy on modules (ALMM).
- **Valuation anchor:** P/B on regulated equity and DCF for regulated utilities; EV/EBITDA and EV per MW for renewables.
- **Trap:** a capacity pipeline valued as commissioned; receivables quality; renewable IRRs assuming stable module costs.

## Real estate

- **Lead KPIs:** pre-sales value and area; realisation per sq ft; collections; launches and pipeline; net debt; unsold inventory months; land bank; rental portfolio (leasing, occupancy).
- **Data releases:** quarterly business updates (pre-sales, in the first week after quarter end for several developers); RERA registrations; home-loan rates (MPC); stamp-duty changes; property consultants' reports (leads).
- **Short-term movers:** pre-sales against guidance; launch success; rate cuts; land deals and joint development agreements (JDAs).
- **Valuation anchor:** NAV (project-level DCF plus land) and P/B; EV/EBITDA for annuity (office) assets.
- **Trap:** pre-sales equated with revenue (P&L recognition lags under Ind AS 115); launches without collections; land valued at optimistic market rates.

## Telecom

- **Lead KPIs:** ARPU; subscriber adds (especially 4G/5G); churn; data usage per subscriber; EBITDA margin; capex; net debt; spectrum and AGR dues.
- **Data releases:** TRAI monthly subscriber data (with a lag); tariff-hike announcements; spectrum auctions; DoT/court rulings on AGR and dues.
- **Short-term movers:** tariff hikes; subscriber data; government relief on dues; fundraising by weaker players.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA; SOTP for towers and African operations.
- **Trap:** treating ARPU hikes as permanent without competitive response; ignoring spectrum-payment obligations in net debt.

## Textiles and apparel

- **Lead KPIs:** cotton and yarn spreads; export orders; utilisation; US/EU retail inventory; working capital; subsidy dependence (RoDTEP, RoSCTL, PLI); **brands:** SSSG and store adds.
- **Data releases:** cotton prices (CAI, MCX); export data; US retail sales and tariff actions; FTA progress (UK, EU).
- **Short-term movers:** US tariff changes against competitors (Bangladesh, Vietnam, China); FTA headlines; cotton-price moves.
- **Valuation anchor:** P/E and EV/EBITDA against cycle margins.
- **Trap:** tariff "beneficiary" narratives without order evidence; margins that depend on export incentives.

## Aviation and logistics

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Airlines:** ASK, RPK, load factor, yield, CASK ex-fuel, ATF cost, grounded fleet, market share, lease liabilities.
  - **Logistics:** volumes (TEU, tonnage), realisation, utilisation, rail/port share.
  - **Airports:** passenger traffic.
- **Data releases:** DGCA monthly traffic (around mid-month); monthly ATF price revisions (1st, by OMCs); port cargo (IPA monthly); rail freight; container volumes; e-way bill counts (GSTN).
- **Short-term movers:** ATF changes; crude; INR (dollar-denominated lease costs); competitor capacity or failures; traffic data.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDAR (airlines); EV/EBITDA and P/E (logistics).
- **Trap:** peak-season yields annualised; ignoring lease liabilities; logistics volume growth from low-margin cargo.

## Sugar and agri

- **Lead KPIs:**
  - **Sugar:** cane crushed, recovery %, sugar and ethanol volumes, FRP (cane price) against the minimum selling price, inventory, export quota.
  - **Agri inputs:** fertiliser subsidy receivables, agrochem channel inventory, seed volumes.
- **Data releases:** ISMA production estimates; government export-quota and ethanol-price notifications (usually around the sugar season from October); FRP announcements; IMD monsoon; sowing data; fertiliser subsidy (NBS) rates.
- **Short-term movers:** export-quota decisions; ethanol-pricing or diversion policy; MSP and FRP changes; monsoon forecasts.
- **Valuation anchor:** EV/EBITDA and P/E against the cycle; replacement value of distillery capacity.
- **Trap:** policy-dependent earnings valued as structural; ignoring inventory valuation effects on sugar P&L.

## Using this file

For long-horizon work, feed these KPIs into [financial normalization](financial-normalization.md) and the [analyst playbook](analyst-playbook.md) earnings-engine step. For 1–10 session trades, the "short-term movers" line tells you which calendar item or data release the [short-term playbook](short-term-playbook.md) must check. A KPI move without a filing or data source is a rumour.
