# Portfolio and opportunity cost

A stock decision is incomplete until compared with what the investor could own instead. Use alternatives with the same horizon, currency, evidence cutoff, cost treatment and scenario basis.

Compare the candidate with cash or a suitable low-risk instrument, a diversified Indian benchmark and the strongest researched peer or existing holding. Show prospective return range, downside, confidence, liquidity, factor and sector exposure, catalyst dependence and tax or friction differences.

When holdings are known, map sector, factor, promoter-group, lender, commodity, currency, regulatory and catalyst concentration before and after the position. Use historical covariance only as a descriptive input and add coherent stress scenarios because correlations can rise in crises.

Personalized sizing requires portfolio value, holdings, approved loss budget and liquidity limit. Size from the smaller of loss capacity, cash capacity, concentration limits and executable liquidity. If those inputs are missing, show formulas and conditional ranges only.
