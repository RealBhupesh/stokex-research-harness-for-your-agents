# Management credibility ledger

Evaluate behavior over time, not presentation quality.

Record dated guidance, capital-allocation promises, capacity plans, acquisition claims, deleveraging targets, dilution statements, related-party commitments and governance assurances. Compare each with the actual outcome on the original deadline and accounting basis.

Distinguish missed external assumptions from controllable execution. Track optimism bias, changing definitions, withdrawn guidance, selective metrics, repeated exceptional items, equity issuance, promoter transactions and treatment of minority shareholders.

Past accuracy informs forecast confidence but never proves integrity. A strong ledger cannot override audit, cash, related-party, legal or disclosure red flags.
